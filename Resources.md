This workshop and all materials were developed at the BC Children's Hospital Research Institute, which is located on the unceded territory of the Coast Salish Peoples, including the territories of the xwməθkwəy̓əm (Musqueam), Skwxwú7mesh (Squamish), Stó:lō and Səl̓ílwətaʔ/Selilwitulh (Tsleil- Waututh) Nations. 

Please take a moment to explore [native-land.ca](https://native-land.ca/) and learn about the Indigenous territories, languages, and treaties in your area.
